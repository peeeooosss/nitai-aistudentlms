## PROGRAM OUTCOMES

By the end of this 1-month program, students will:

1. **AI Literacy** — Understand AI fundamentals, LLMs, and how AI is transforming every industry.
2. **Prompt Engineering** — Write effective prompts, chain prompts, and use advanced prompting patterns to get consistent, high-quality AI outputs.
3. **AI Content Creation** — Create blogs, social media posts, emails, newsletters, video scripts, and marketing copy using AI tools at professional quality.
4. **AI Visual Design** — Generate images, brand assets, logos, and visual content using Midjourney, DALL·E, and other AI image tools.
5. **AI Audio & Video** — Produce voiceovers, AI-generated audio, and basic video scripts/edits.
6. **AI for Business** — Automate research, data analysis, spreadsheets, and lead generation using AI.
7. **Freelancing Readiness** — Build a personal brand, portfolio, and service offerings. Price and sell AI services on freelance platforms.
8. **Ethical AI Use** — Understand responsible AI usage, bias, and transparency in AI-generated content.
