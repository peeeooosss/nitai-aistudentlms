## Quiz Study Guide

The Phase 1 certification quiz covers the full 30-day curriculum. Here are the key areas to study.

### Must-Know Definitions

- **LLM**: Large Language Model — AI trained on massive text datasets that generates human-like text
- **Prompt Engineering**: The practice of crafting inputs to AI models to get desired outputs
- **Temperature**: A parameter controlling AI output randomness (low = focused, high = creative)
- **Context Window**: The maximum amount of text an AI model can process at once
- **Hallucination**: When AI generates confident but factually incorrect information
- **Few-Shot Prompting**: Providing examples in your prompt to guide AI output
- **Chain of Thought**: Prompting AI to show its reasoning step by step
- **Value-Based Pricing**: Setting prices based on the value delivered to the client, not time spent
- **Lead Magnet**: A free resource offered in exchange for contact information
- **Scope Creep**: When project requirements expand beyond the original agreement without adjustments to timeline or budget

### Must-Know Frameworks

Memorize these frameworks and be able to apply them to scenarios:

1. **Prompt Engineering Framework**: Context → Task → Format → Constraints
2. **Brand Equation**: Expertise + Consistency + Visibility + Trust
3. **Trust Stack**: Knowledge → Results → Process → Social Proof → Consistency
4. **Lead Generation Funnel**: Awareness → Interest → Evaluation → Conversion
5. **Personalization Pyramid**: Name → Role → Company → Trigger → Insight
6. **Client Communication Lifecycle**: Contact → Discovery → Proposal → Execution → Delivery
7. **Discovery Framework**: Problem, Impact, Vision, Constraints, Stakeholders
8. **Pricing Models**: Hourly, Project, Value-Based, Retainer
9. **Three-Tier Packages**: Starter, Standard, Premium
10. **Ethical Principles**: Transparency, Accuracy/Accountability, Privacy, Fairness, IP Respect
11. **Content Workflow**: Ideation → Drafting → Editing → Publishing → Analysis
12. **Automation Design**: Trigger → Action → Condition → Output

### Common Quiz Scenarios

Be prepared for questions like:

- "A client asks you to use their confidential data in an AI tool. What do you do?"
- "You notice AI-generated code has a subtle bug. How do you handle it?"
- "A client says your price is too high. Write your response."
- "How would you structure a discovery call for an AI automation project?"
- "A prospect asks: 'Why should I hire you instead of doing this myself with ChatGPT?' Write your answer."
- "Build a prompt that would generate a professional proposal for an AI consulting project."
- "What are three signs that a lead is ready to buy?"
- "How do you handle scope creep during a project?"
