## Session Preparation: Graduation Presentation

### What You've Built Over 90 Days

Over the past three months, you have:

1. **Mastered AI Fundamentals** — From prompt engineering to machine learning, you understand the technology landscape
2. **Built Real AI Projects** — Multiple hands-on projects demonstrating practical skills
3. **Developed Business Acumen** — Business models, revenue strategies, and financial analysis
4. **Learned Enterprise Skills** — Strategy, governance, security, team leadership, and stakeholder management
5. **Created Your Business Plan** — A comprehensive AI franchise or business concept ready for launch

### Graduation Presentation Format

Each graduate will deliver a **7-minute presentation** followed by **3 minutes of Q&A**.

**Presentation Structure:**

```
Slide 1: The Problem (1 minute)
├── What pain point are you solving?
├── Who experiences this problem?
└── What is the cost of the problem?

Slide 2: The Solution (1 minute)
├── What AI capability addresses this problem?
├── How does it work (in simple terms)?
└── What makes it different from alternatives?

Slide 3: Business Model (1 minute)
├── How do you make money?
├── Who is your target customer?
└── What is your pricing model?

Slide 4: Market Opportunity (1 minute)
├── How big is the market?
├── What is your addressable segment?
└── Who are your competitors and how do you differentiate?

Slide 5: Traction & Validation (1 minute)
├── What have you built or validated?
├── What feedback have you received from potential customers?
└── What evidence supports your business thesis?

Slide 6: Financial Projections (1 minute)
├── 3-year revenue projection
├── Key assumptions and unit economics
└── Break-even timeline

Slide 7: The Ask & Next Steps (1 minute)
├── What do you need to launch?
├── What is your 90-day post-graduation plan?
└── What support would be most valuable?

+ Q&A (3 minutes)
```

### Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Problem Clarity | 15% | Is the problem compelling and well-articulated? |
| Solution Viability | 20% | Is the AI solution technically feasible and differentiated? |
| Business Model | 20% | Is the revenue model sound and scalable? |
| Market Understanding | 15% | Does the presenter understand the competitive landscape? |
| Financial Rigor | 15% | Are projections realistic with clear assumptions? |
| Presentation Quality | 15% | Clear, confident, and within time limit |

## Live Session Activities

### Activity 1: Peer Presentations

Present your business concept to the cohort. Each presentation is 7 minutes + 3 minutes Q&A. Peers provide structured feedback using the evaluation criteria.

### Activity 2: Franchise Access Briefing

Learn about the franchise opportunity:

**Franchise Benefits:**
- Access to the AI platform and technology stack
- Training and certification program
- Marketing support and lead generation
- Ongoing education and community
- Brand association and credibility

**Franchise Requirements:**
- Commitment to quality standards
- Investment in training and certification
- Active participation in the franchise community
- Quarterly business reviews and reporting
- Adherence to brand and compliance standards

### Activity 3: Alumni Network Launch

**Post-graduation community includes:**
- Private Slack/Discord channel for alumni
- Monthly virtual meetups and guest speakers
- Annual alumni conference
- Peer mentorship matching
- Deal-sharing and collaboration platform
- Continuing education resources

### Activity 4: 90-Day Post-Graduation Action Plan

Create a concrete plan for the next 90 days after graduation:

**Days 1-30: Foundation**
- Finalize business plan details
- Secure initial funding or commitment
- Begin customer discovery conversations
- Set up business infrastructure
- Join franchise community and begin onboarding

**Days 31-60: Validation**
- Conduct 20+ customer discovery interviews
- Build or refine MVP based on feedback
- Secure 3-5 pilot customers
- Complete franchise training module 1
- Establish advisory board

**Days 61-90: Launch**
- Launch with first paying customers
- Refine pricing based on early feedback
- Build repeatable sales process
- Complete franchise certification
- Plan for scaling (team, technology, operations)
