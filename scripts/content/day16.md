- Understand AI voice generation technology and platforms

## The AI Voice Revolution

AI voice technology has reached near-human quality. Tools like ElevenLabs, Play.ht, and WellSaid Labs produce voiceovers that are indistinguishable from human recordings for most use cases.

### When AI Voiceovers Make Sense
- **Explainer videos:** Consistent, professional narration
- **Course content:** Scalable training material production
- **Podcast intros/outros:** Branded audio elements
- **E-learning:** Automated narration for training modules
- **Social media:** Voiceover for short-form video content
- **Multilingual content:** Translate and dub into other languages

### When to Use Human Voice Actors
- **Emotional storytelling:** Deep emotional connection requires human nuance
- **Character work:** Fictional characters with distinct personalities
- **Brand mascots:** Consistent voice identity over years
- **High-budget productions:** Film, TV, major advertising campaigns

## Prompting for Natural Voiceovers

### Script Structure for Voiceover
```
[Narrator voice: warm, professional, conversational]

Welcome to [topic]. I am [name], and today we are going to explore
[what they will learn].

[pause]

Here is what you need to know.

[pause]

[Main content section]

Let me break that down for you.

[pause]

[Supporting details]

And that is [summary statement].

[pause]

Thanks for watching. If this was helpful, hit subscribe
and I will see you in the next one.
```

### Voice Direction Prompts
```
Write a 60-second voiceover script about [topic].

Voice direction:
- Tone: [warm and friendly / professional and authoritative / energetic and exciting]
- Pace: [conversational / measured / fast-paced]
- Audience: [beginners / professionals / general public]
- Style: [educational / promotional / storytelling]

Include:
- Natural pauses marked with [pause]
- Emphasis cues marked with [emphasis on: word]
- Breathing room between key points
- Conversational transitions ("Here is the thing...", "Let me break that down...")
```

### Platform-Specific Prompts

**YouTube Voiceover:**
```
Write a voiceover script for a 5-minute YouTube video about [topic].

Structure:
- Hook (0-15 seconds): Attention-grabbing opening
- Intro (15-30 seconds): What they will learn
- Main content (30-240 seconds): Key points with examples
- Recap (240-270 seconds): Summary of takeaways
- CTA (270-300 seconds): Subscribe, next video

Style: Conversational, like talking to a friend who wants to learn
Pace: Moderate, with natural pauses for emphasis
```

**TikTok/Reels Voiceover:**
```
Write a 30-second voiceover for a TikTok about [topic].

Structure:
- Hook (0-3 seconds): Bold opening statement
- Content (3-25 seconds): Fast-paced, one key point
- CTA (25-30 seconds): Follow, save, comment

Style: Energetic, direct, like talking to a peer
Pace: Quick but clear
Include: On-screen text cues in [brackets]
```

## Audio Production Workflow

### Step 1: Script Writing (10 minutes)
Use AI to generate the script, then edit for natural speech patterns:
- Shorter sentences than written content
- Conversational transitions
- Emphasis cues for important points
- Pause markers for pacing

### Step 2: Voice Generation (5 minutes)
```
1. Paste script into AI voice tool
2. Select voice that matches your brand
3. Adjust speed (0.9x for calm, 1.1x for energetic)
4. Generate and listen to first draft
5. Regenerate problem sections with adjusted pronunciation
```

### Step 3: Audio Editing (15 minutes)
```
1. Import generated audio into editing tool (Audacity, Descript, Adobe Audition)
2. Remove background noise if needed
3. Adjust volume levels (target -6dB to -3dB peaks)
4. Add music bed (10-15% volume under voice)
5. Add sound effects for transitions if appropriate
6. Export as MP3 (podcast) or WAV (video production)
```

### Step 4: Synchronization (10 minutes)
```
1. Import audio into video editor
2. Align voiceover with visuals
3. Adjust timing for key moments
4. Add captions/subtitles (critical for accessibility)
5. Final review and export
```

## Quality Checklist

Before publishing any AI voiceover:
- [ ] Script reads naturally when spoken aloud
- [ ] Pronunciation of technical terms is correct
- [ ] Pacing feels natural, not robotic
- [ ] Music/effects do not overpower the voice
- [ ] Audio levels are consistent throughout
- [ ] Captions match the audio exactly
- [ ] The overall feel matches your brand voice

## Key Takeaways

- AI voice generation has reached near-human quality for most use cases
- ElevenLabs leads in naturalness; choose platforms based on your specific needs
- Script structure for voiceover differs from written content: shorter sentences, conversational transitions, pause markers
- The production workflow is: script, generate, edit, synchronize
- Always add captions — most social media video is watched without sound
