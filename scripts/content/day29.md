## Setting Up for Payment

Before you can earn, you need the infrastructure to receive payment. Here is your checklist.

### Payment Platforms

Choose at least one platform for receiving client payments:

- **PayPal**: Widely accepted, easy to set up, good for international payments (fees: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction)
- **Stripe**: Professional payment processing, integrates with websites and proposals (fees: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction)
- **Wise (TransferWise)**: Best for international payments with lower fees and better exchange rates
- **Bank wire transfer**: For larger projects, direct bank transfers have lower percentage fees

### Invoicing Setup

Professional invoicing signals that you are a real business. Set up:

1. **Invoice template**: Create a professional invoice with your brand, contact info, payment terms, and bank details
2. **Invoice numbering system**: Sequential numbers (INV-001, INV-002) for easy tracking
3. **Payment terms**: Define when payment is due (Net 15 or Net 30 are standard)
4. **Late payment policy**: State what happens if payment is late (e.g., 1.5% monthly interest)

Use AI to generate your invoice template and payment terms language.

### Tax Preparation

As a freelancer, you are responsible for your own taxes. Take these steps now:

- **Separate bank account**: Keep business income and expenses in a dedicated account
- **Track all expenses**: AI tools, subscriptions, internet, home office — these are deductible
- **Set aside tax money**: Move 25-30% of every payment into a savings account for taxes
- **Understand your obligations**: Research self-employment tax requirements in your jurisdiction
- **Consider an accountant**: For your first year, professional tax advice pays for itself

## Your First Revenue Targets

The transition from learning to earning requires concrete targets.

### 30-Day Revenue Target

Set a specific, achievable revenue goal for your first 30 days of active freelancing:

- **Conservative**: $500 (2 small projects at $250 each)
- **Moderate**: $1,500 (1 standard project or 3 small projects)
- **Ambitious**: $5,000 (1 premium project or multiple standard projects)

Choose the target that stretches you without overwhelming you. Write it down and put it somewhere you see daily.

### Activity Targets

Revenue follows activity. Track these leading indicators:

- Outreach messages sent per week: 20
- Conversations started per week: 5
- Proposals sent per week: 3
- Discovery calls completed per week: 2
- Projects closed per month: 1-2

If you hit these activity targets consistently, the revenue follows.

### Milestone Celebrations

Set specific milestones and celebrate them:

- **First outreach message sent**: You are in the game
- **First conversation started**: Someone is interested
- **First proposal sent**: You are officially selling
- **First client call**: You are in the running
- **First project closed**: You are earning
- **First payment received**: You are a professional

Each milestone deserves recognition. This is hard work, and acknowledging progress sustains motivation.

## Transitioning to Phase 2

Phase 2 shifts from building foundations to scaling your practice. Here is what to expect:

### Phase 2 Focus Areas

- **Scaling client acquisition**: Moving from manual outreach to systematic growth
- **Advanced AI applications**: Deeper technical skills and more complex projects
- **Business systems**: Building processes that run without your constant attention
- **Income optimization**: Moving from project work to recurring revenue
- **Team building**: Considering when and how to subcontract or hire

### Preparing Now

Start these habits during the remaining days of Phase 1:

- **Daily outreach**: Send at least 3 personalized messages per day
- **Weekly review**: Every Friday, review your numbers and adjust your approach
- **Monthly portfolio update**: Add new projects, refresh descriptions
- **Continuous learning**: Spend 30 minutes daily learning new AI tools and techniques
- **Network building**: Connect with one new person in your industry every day
