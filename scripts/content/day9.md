# Blog Writing with AI Assistants

## Learning Objectives
- Master AI-assisted blog writing from research to publication
- Learn to maintain authentic voice while leveraging AI speed
- Build systems for consistent, high-quality blog output
- Understand SEO basics for AI-generated content

---

## Why Blogging Still Matters in 2026

Despite the rise of video, podcasts, and social media, blogging remains one of the most effective content strategies:

- **SEO value:** Blog posts drive organic traffic for years (evergreen content)
- **Authority building:** Long-form content establishes expertise
- **Lead generation:** Each post is a new entry point to your funnel
- **Content repurposing:** One blog post becomes social posts, newsletters, videos, and more
- **owned media:** Your blog is content you control — not subject to algorithm changes

AI makes blogging accessible to everyone. You do not need to be a professional writer to produce valuable, engaging blog content.

## The AI Blog Writing Workflow

### Step 1: Topic Research (10 minutes)
```
Prompt: I want to write a blog post about [topic].

Research and provide:
1. The top 5 questions people ask about this topic (from Google's "People Also Ask")
2. 3 statistics that would surprise or inform readers
3. The most common mistakes people make with [topic]
4. What the top-ranking articles cover well
5. What they miss or could improve on

Format as a research brief I can use to write the post.
```

### Step 2: Outline Creation (5 minutes)
```
Based on this research: [paste research brief]

Create a blog post outline:
- Title options (give me 5, I will pick the best)
- Target word count: 1200-1500 words
- 5-7 sections with H2 headers
- 2-3 sub-points per section
- Introduction hook and conclusion CTA
- Internal linking opportunities

The post should be practical and actionable, not theoretical.
```

### Step 3: Drafting (20 minutes)
Write the post section by section. For each section:

```
Write the "[Section Title]" section of a blog post about [topic].

Context: [what came before this section]
Next section will cover: [what comes after]

Requirements:
- 150-250 words
- Include at least one concrete example
- Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Active voice throughout
- Tone: [your brand voice — casual/professional/technical]
```

### Step 4: Human Editing Pass (15 minutes)
This is where you add value that AI cannot:
1. Insert personal anecdotes and opinions
2. Verify all facts and statistics
3. Replace generic AI language with your unique voice
4. Add specific examples from your experience
5. Cut unnecessary filler (AI tends to pad)

### Step 5: SEO and Polish (10 minutes)
```
Optimize this blog post for SEO:

Target keyword: [keyword]
Secondary keywords: [list 2-3 related terms]

Check and provide:
1. Optimized title (50-60 chars)
2. Meta description (150-160 chars)
3. Header structure (H1, H2, H3)
4. Keyword placement recommendations
5. Image alt text suggestions (for 3 images)
6. Internal link suggestions
```

## Blog Post Formats That Work

### How-To Guide
- Problem statement
- Step-by-step instructions
- Examples at each step
- Common mistakes to avoid
- Summary checklist

### Listicle
- Compelling title with number
- Brief intro setting context
- Each item has: heading, explanation, example
- Brief conclusion tying it together

### Case Study
- Situation/context
- Challenge/problem
- Solution/approach
- Results/outcomes
- Lessons learned

### Comparison
- What are we comparing and why
- Feature-by-feature analysis
- Pros and cons of each
- Recommendation based on use case

## Maintaining Your Voice

The biggest challenge with AI blog writing is producing content that sounds like you, not a machine.

### Voice Anchors
Define 3-5 voice characteristics and reference them in every prompt:
```
My writing voice:
1. Conversational but informed — like explaining to a smart friend
2. Opinionated — I take stances, not just present options
3. Example-heavy — every concept gets a real-world example
4. Concise — no padding, every sentence earns its place
5. Slightly irreverent — I use humor when appropriate
```

### Before/After Example

**AI Draft (generic):**
"It is important to note that social media marketing can be an effective way to reach your target audience and build brand awareness. There are several strategies that businesses can implement to improve their social media presence."

**After Human Edit (voice added):**
"Here is the thing about social media marketing: everyone knows they should do it, but most businesses are just posting into the void. The difference between a social media strategy that generates leads and one that wastes your time comes down to three specific tactics."

## Quality Checklist

Before publishing any blog post:
- [ ] Title is clear, specific, and includes target keyword
- [ ] Introduction hooks the reader in the first 2 sentences
- [ ] Each section delivers on its heading's promise
- [ ] At least 3 concrete examples throughout
- [ ] All statistics are fact-checked and sourced
- [ ] Conclusion includes a clear call-to-action
- [ ] Meta description is compelling and under 160 characters
- [ ] You would actually want to read this post yourself

## Key Takeaways

- AI accelerates blog writing but requires human editing for voice, accuracy, and authenticity
- Follow the 5-step workflow: research, outline, draft, human edit, SEO polish
- Define your voice anchors and reference them in every AI prompt
- Blog posts are long-term assets — invest in quality over speed
- One well-written blog post can be repurposed into 10+ pieces of content
