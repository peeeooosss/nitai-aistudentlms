## Session Preparation: AI Revenue Models

### The Recurring Revenue Imperative

One-time project revenue is unpredictable and doesn't scale. AI businesses must transition to recurring revenue models to achieve sustainable growth and enterprise valuations.

### Revenue Model Spectrum for AI

| Model | Revenue Type | Predictability | Scalability | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consulting/Projects | One-time | Low | Low | Custom AI model development |
| Managed Services | Recurring (time-based) | Medium | Medium | Running and maintaining AI systems |
| Platform/SaaS | Recurring (subscription) | High | High | AI platform with monthly subscription |
| Usage-Based | Variable recurring | Medium | Very High | Pay per API call or prediction |
| Outcome-Based | Performance-based | Low | High | Fee tied to business outcomes |
| Hybrid | Combination | Medium-High | High | Platform fee + usage + outcome bonus |

### SaaS Metrics for AI Products

**Core SaaS Metrics:**

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **MRR** (Monthly Recurring Revenue) | Sum of monthly subscription fees | Growing 15-20% MoM early stage |
| **ARR** (Annual Recurring Revenue) | MRR × 12 | — |
| **ARPU** (Average Revenue Per User) | MRR / Number of customers | Growing over time |
| **Churn Rate** | Customers lost / Total customers | < 5% monthly for B2B |
| **Net Revenue Retention** | (Starting MRR + expansion - churn - contraction) / Starting MRR | > 110% (expansion > churn) |
| **LTV** (Customer Lifetime Value) | ARPU / Monthly churn rate | > 3× CAC |
| **CAC** (Customer Acquisition Cost) | Total sales & marketing cost / New customers | Payback < 18 months |
| **LTV:CAC Ratio** | LTV / CAC | > 3:1 |
| **Rule of 40** | Revenue growth rate + profit margin | > 40% |

### AI-Specific Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| API Calls per Customer | Volume of AI predictions/requests | Usage indicates value extraction |
| Model Usage Depth | Features used vs. available | Deeper usage → stickier product |
| Data Volume Growth | Amount of data processed per customer | More data → better models → more value |
| Time to First Value | Days from signup to first meaningful result | Faster → better retention |
| Expansion Revenue % | Revenue from existing customers growing their usage | Net negative churn indicator |

## Live Exercises

### Exercise 1: Pricing Strategy Design

**Scenario:** You've built an AI-powered document processing platform. You need to set pricing. Your costs:
- Infrastructure: $5 per 1,000 documents processed
- AI API costs: $2 per 1,000 documents
- Support and overhead: $3 per 1,000 documents
- Total COGS: $10 per 1,000 documents

**Customer segments:**
- Small business: 1,000-10,000 documents/month
- Mid-market: 10,000-100,000 documents/month
- Enterprise: 100,000-1,000,000 documents/month

**Your task (groups of 3-4):**
1. Design three pricing tiers (features, limits, prices)
2. Choose between: flat-rate subscription, usage-based, or hybrid
3. Calculate unit economics for each segment
4. Design an expansion revenue strategy
5. Present your pricing with justification

### Exercise 2: Churn Prevention Playbook

**Scenario:** Your AI SaaS product has:
- 100 customers, $50K MRR
- 5% monthly churn (losing 5 customers/month)
- Average customer lifetime: 20 months
- LTV:CAC ratio: 2:1 (below target of 3:1)

**Your task:**
1. Identify the top 5 reasons AI SaaS products churn
2. Design early warning signals for each churn reason
3. Create a churn prevention playbook with specific actions
4. Model the impact of reducing churn from 5% to 3% on MRR and LTV

### Exercise 3: Revenue Growth Model

**Scenario:** You're building a revenue model for an AI platform startup. Current state: 20 customers, $20K MRR, growing 10% MoM.

**Model the following scenarios for the next 12 months:**
1. Organic growth only (current rate)
2. Organic + 2 new customers/month from marketing
3. Organic + marketing + channel partnerships
4. All channels + international expansion at month 6

For each scenario, calculate: MRR, ARR, number of customers, LTV:CAC, and runway needed.

## Discussion Topics

### Topic 1: Usage-Based vs. Subscription Pricing

The AI industry is debating whether to use pure usage-based pricing (like an API) or subscription-based pricing (like SaaS). What are the tradeoffs? When is each appropriate? How do hybrid models work?

### Topic 2: Outcome-Based Pricing

Some AI companies are experimenting with pricing tied to business outcomes (e.g., charge based on revenue increase or cost savings). What are the risks and rewards? How do you attribute outcomes to your AI?

### Topic 3: Expansion Revenue in AI

How do you systematically grow revenue from existing customers? What expansion levers exist in AI products (more usage, more features, more departments, more use cases)?

---

## Key Takeaways

1. Recurring revenue models (SaaS, managed services) are essential for sustainable AI businesses
2. Core SaaS metrics (MRR, churn, LTV, CAC) apply to AI products with AI-specific additions
3. Pricing strategies should balance customer value with unit economics
4. Net revenue retention above 110% indicates strong product-market fit
5. Churn prevention requires understanding AI-specific churn reasons
6. Revenue modeling should incorporate multiple growth channels
