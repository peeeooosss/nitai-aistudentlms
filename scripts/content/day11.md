# Email Marketing with AI

## Learning Objectives
- Build an AI-powered email marketing workflow
- Write high-converting email sequences with AI assistance
- Master subject lines, body copy, and calls-to-action
- Set up automated email funnels using AI tools

---

## Why Email Marketing Still Dominates

Email marketing delivers the highest ROI of any marketing channel — approximately $36 for every $1 spent. Unlike social media, you own your email list. No algorithm changes can take it away.

### The Email Marketing Stack
- **Email platform:** Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Beehiiv, Substack
- **AI writing assistant:** ChatGPT, Claude for copywriting
- **Analytics:** Platform built-in + Google Analytics
- **Automation:** Platform workflows + AI triggers

## Types of Marketing Emails

### Welcome Sequence (5-7 emails)
Sent when someone joins your list. This is your highest-engagement moment.

```
Email 1 (Immediate): Welcome + deliver promised lead magnet
Email 2 (Day 2): Your story — why you do what you do
Email 3 (Day 4): Best content — share your most helpful resource
Email 4 (Day 6): Social proof — testimonials, case studies
Email 5 (Day 8): Soft pitch — introduce your product/service
Email 6 (Day 10): FAQ — address common objections
Email 7 (Day 12): Final pitch + urgency
```

### Newsletter Template
```
Subject line: [curiosity gap or benefit]

Opening (2-3 sentences):
- Personal hook or observation
- Why this matters to the reader

Main content (3-5 paragraphs):
- One main idea per email
- Concrete examples and actionable advice
- Supporting data or case study

Closing (2-3 sentences):
- Summary of the key takeaway
- Clear call-to-action
- P.S. line (often the most-read part)
```

### Promotional Email
```
Subject: [benefit-focused, not salesy]

Opening: Identify the problem your product solves
Body: Explain the solution and its benefits
Social proof: Testimonials or results
Offer: Clear, specific, time-limited
CTA: One clear action to take
Urgency: Why now (without being manipulative)
```

## AI Email Writing Prompts

### Welcome Email
```
Write a welcome email for new subscribers to [your newsletter/business].

Context:
- What they signed up for: [lead magnet or newsletter topic]
- Your expertise: [your niche/authority]
- Your voice: [casual/professional/warm]

Requirements:
- Deliver the promised resource (link placeholder)
- Share a quick personal story that builds connection
- Set expectations for what they will receive
- Ask one question to encourage replies
- Length: 150-200 words
```

### Subject Line Generation
```
Generate 10 subject lines for an email about [topic].

Mix these styles:
- Curiosity gaps ("The one thing most people miss...")
- Benefit-driven ("How to [achieve result] in [timeframe]")
- Question-based ("Are you making this [topic] mistake?")
- Number-based ("3 [topic] strategies that actually work")
- Urgency ("Last chance: [offer] expires tonight")

For each, rate click probability (1-5) and explain why.
```

### Newsletter Draft
```
Write a newsletter email about [topic].

Structure:
1. Personal opening anecdote or observation (2-3 sentences)
2. Main insight or lesson (the core value)
3. Practical application (what to do with this information)
4. Case study or example (proof it works)
5. Clear next step for the reader

Tone: [describe your voice]
Length: 300-400 words
Include: 1 CTA button text suggestion
```

## Email Sequence Automation

### The Nurture Sequence Framework
```
Trigger: User joins list
|
+-> Email 1: Welcome (immediate)
|     Deliver value, set expectations
|
+-> Email 2: Education (Day 3)
|     Teach something useful
|
+-> Email 3: Engagement (Day 6)
|     Ask a question, encourage reply
|
+-> Email 4: Social Proof (Day 9)
|     Share success story
|
+-> Email 5: Soft Sell (Day 12)
|     Introduce your solution
|
+-> Email 6: Hard Sell (Day 14)
|     Clear offer with deadline
|
+-> Email 7: Last Chance (Day 16)
|     Final reminder + bonus
```

### Re-Engagement Sequence
For subscribers who have not opened emails in 30+ days:

```
Subject: "Still interested in [topic]?"

Hey [name],

I noticed you have not opened my emails lately. No hard feelings — inboxes get overwhelming.

I want to make sure I am sending you stuff you actually want to read.

Quick question: What is your biggest challenge with [topic] right now?

Just reply with one word or sentence. I read every response.

[Your name]
```

## Subject Line Mastery

### The Anatomy of a Great Subject Line
- **Length:** 30-50 characters (mobile-friendly)
- **Specificity:** Numbers and specifics outperform vague claims
- **Curiosity:** Open a loop the reader wants to close
- **Benefit:** What will they get by opening?
- **Urgency:** When appropriate, time pressure works

### Subject Line Frameworks
```
1. The Curiosity Gap: "I was wrong about [topic]"
2. The Number: "7 [topic] mistakes I see every day"
3. The Question: "What if [desirable outcome] was easier than you think?"
4. The How-To: "How to [achieve result] without [pain point]"
5. The Story: "The [topic] strategy that changed everything"
6. The Urgency: "[Topic] deadline: [specific time]"
```

## Measuring Email Performance

### Key Metrics
- **Open rate:** % of recipients who opened (industry avg: 20-25%)
- **Click-through rate:** % who clicked a link (industry avg: 2-5%)
- **Conversion rate:** % who took desired action (varies by offer)
- **Unsubscribe rate:** % who left (keep under 0.5% per email)
- **Reply rate:** % who replied (great for deliverability)

### AI Performance Analysis
```
Here are my email metrics for the last 5 campaigns:

Campaign 1: Open 22%, CTR 3.1%, Conv 1.2%
Campaign 2: Open 18%, CTR 2.8%, Conv 0.9%
[continue with your data]

Analyze:
1. What patterns do you see?
2. Which subject lines performed best and why?
3. Which content drove the most clicks?
4. What should I test next?
5. Predict next month's performance if I [proposed change]
```

## Key Takeaways

- Email marketing has the highest ROI of any channel — AI makes it faster and more consistent
- Use sequences (welcome, nurture, re-engagement) to automate your relationship building
- Subject lines determine whether your email gets read — test multiple versions
- AI excels at drafting and analysis; human input is needed for personal stories and strategy
- Measure open rates, CTR, and conversions — then iterate
