## Study Material: Comprehensive Program Review

**Phase 1 (Days 1-30): Foundations**
- AI fundamentals and core concepts

- Hands-on projects building AI tools

**Phase 2 (Days 31-60): Applied AI**
- Advanced AI techniques (computer vision, NLP, generative AI)

**Phase 3 (Days 61-90): Enterprise & Launch**
- Enterprise AI strategy and architecture
- Security, compliance, and governance
- Team leadership and project management
- ROI measurement and stakeholder communication
- Franchise model, partnerships, and scaling

**Technical Readiness:**

| Component | Status | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Core AI capability | ☐ Demonstrated | Working prototype or production system |

**Business Readiness:**

| Component | Status | Target |
|---|---|---|

| Insurance | ☐ Obtained | Appropriate coverage in place |

**Operational Readiness:**

| Component | Status | Target |
|---|---|---|

| Partnerships | ☐ Established | Key partnerships secured |
| Quality | ☐ Measured | Quality metrics and monitoring in place |

**Strategic Readiness:**

| Component | Status | Target |
|---|---|---|

| Competitive position | ☐ Analyzed | Differentiation clear and defensible |

| Exit/long-term vision | ☐ Articulated | 3-5 year vision clear |

## Practice Challenge: Final Self-Assessment

### Part 1: Knowledge Validation

Answer these questions without referencing course materials:

1. Name the five pillars of enterprise AI strategy.
2. What are the three horizons of AI transformation?
3. What is the formula for calculating AI ROI?
4. Name three privacy-preserving AI techniques.
5. What is the difference between HITL, hotL, and hotOL decision models?
6. What metrics should be tracked in an AI SaaS business?
7. What are the key components of an AI franchise model?
8. Name three AI-specific security threats.
9. What is the recommended approach to AI project estimation?
10. How do you measure AI project success?

### Part 2: Application Exercise

**Design a complete AI business plan (2-3 pages) for a franchise model:**

1. **Business Concept** — What AI service will you franchise?
2. **Target Market** — Who are your ideal franchisees and end customers?
3. **Technology Platform** — What AI capabilities will the platform provide?
4. **Franchise Model** — Economics, support, and standards
5. **Go-to-Market** — How will you acquire your first 10 franchisees?
6. **Financial Projections** — 3-year revenue model
7. **Launch Plan** — What needs to happen in the next 30 days?

### Part 3: Gap Analysis

Based on your self-assessment and the knowledge validation:
1. Identify your top 3 strength areas
2. Identify your top 3 areas for improvement
3. Create a 30-day development plan for each improvement area
4. Identify resources (people, courses, tools) for continued growth
