## The Enterprise AI Sales Landscape

Selling AI solutions in enterprise environments is fundamentally different from selling traditional software. The sales cycle is longer, the buyer committee is larger, and the concerns are more complex.

### The Enterprise AI Buyer

**Buyer Committee Composition:**

| Role | Primary Concern | Objection Style | Influences |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Executive Sponsor** | Business outcomes, competitive advantage | "Will this actually work?" | Budget, priority |
| **Technical Evaluator** | Architecture, integration, scalability | "How does this actually work?" | Technical feasibility |
| **Financial Buyer (CFO)** | ROI, cost justification, risk | "What's the total cost?" | Budget approval |
| **Legal/Compliance** | Regulatory, liability, data privacy | "Is this legal/safe?" | Deal blocking |
| **End User Champion** | Ease of use, workflow improvement | "Will my team actually use this?" | Adoption |
| **IT/Security** | Infrastructure, security, support | "Can we support this?" | Deployment |

### The Enterprise AI Sales Cycle

```
Stage 1: PROSPECTING & QUALIFICATION (2-4 weeks)
├── Identify target accounts
├── Research pain points and AI readiness
├── Initial outreach and relationship building
├── Qualify opportunity (BANT + AI readiness)
└── Output: Qualified opportunity in pipeline

Stage 2: DISCOVERY & ASSESSMENT (2-4 weeks)
├── Deep-dive on business problems
├── Current state assessment
├── Data readiness evaluation
├── Stakeholder mapping
├── Success criteria definition
└── Output: Discovery document and mutual action plan

Stage 3: SOLUTION DESIGN (2-4 weeks)
├── Tailored solution architecture
├── Use case prioritization
├── Technical proof of concept plan
├── ROI modeling
└── Output: Solution proposal and business case

Stage 4: PROOF OF VALUE (4-8 weeks)
├── Technical POC with real data
├── Business case validation
├── Reference customer connections
├── Security and compliance review
└── Output: POC results and validation report

Stage 5: NEGOTIATION & CLOSE (2-4 weeks)
├── Commercial proposal
├── Contract negotiation
├── Legal and procurement
├── Executive alignment
└── Output: Signed contract

Total typical timeline: 12-24 weeks
```

### Building the AI Business Case

The business case is the most critical document in an enterprise AI sale. It must be rigorous enough for a CFO and accessible enough for a CEO.

**Business Case Template:**

```
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 page)
├── Problem statement
├── Proposed solution
├── Expected outcomes
├── Investment required
└── Timeline and ROI

CURRENT STATE ASSESSMENT (2-3 pages)
├── Business process overview
├── Pain points and costs
├── Current tools and limitations
├── Data availability and quality
└── Competitive landscape

PROPOSED SOLUTION (2-3 pages)
├── AI approach and methodology
├── Architecture and integration
├── Implementation plan
├── Technology stack
└── Team and governance

BUSINESS IMPACT (2-3 pages)
├── Cost savings analysis
├── Revenue impact
├── Risk reduction
├── Productivity gains
├── Customer experience improvements

FINANCIAL ANALYSIS (1-2 pages)
├── Total cost of ownership (3 years)
├── ROI calculation with methodology
├── NPV and payback period
├── Sensitivity analysis
└── Comparison with alternative approaches

RISK ASSESSMENT (1 page)
├── Implementation risks
├── Mitigation strategies
├── Contingency plans
└── Success metrics and thresholds

APPENDIX
├── Technical architecture diagrams
├── Data requirements
├── Compliance considerations
├── Reference case studies
└── Team bios and qualifications
```

## Project Assignment: AI Sales Playbook

### Scenario

You are the VP of Sales at an AI consulting and solutions company. Your company specializes in helping enterprises implement AI across operations. You need to create a comprehensive sales playbook that your team of 15 sales professionals will use.

### Your Deliverable: The Enterprise AI Sales Playbook

Create a complete sales playbook covering:

**1. Market Segmentation (3-4 pages)**
- Define 4 target verticals with specific AI use cases for each
- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) for each vertical
- Buying triggers (events that create AI buying urgency)
- Competitive landscape by vertical

**2. Sales Methodology (4-5 pages)**
- Qualification framework adapted for AI sales
- Discovery question bank (20+ questions)
- Objection handling guide (15+ objections with responses)
- Competitive differentiation strategies
- Pricing and packaging models

**3. Sales Collateral (3-4 pages)**
- Elevator pitch (30-second and 2-minute versions)
- ROI calculator design
- Demo script for flagship solution
- Case study template
- Executive presentation template

**4. Process and Tools (2-3 pages)**
- CRM pipeline stages and exit criteria
- Salesforce/HubSpot workflow configuration
- Sales-to-handoff process to delivery team
- Commission structure for AI solutions
- Forecasting methodology

**5. Training Plan (2-3 pages)**
- Onboarding curriculum for new sales hires
- Ongoing education on AI developments
- Role-play scenarios and practice scripts
- Certification requirements

### Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | What We're Looking For |
|---|---|---|
| Completeness | 25% | Covers all aspects of the sales process |
| Practicality | 25% | Actionable, not theoretical |
| AI-Specific | 20% | Addresses unique challenges of selling AI |
| Business Acumen | 20% | Demonstrates understanding of enterprise buying |
| Creativity | 10% | Innovative approaches to AI sales challenges |

## Key Takeaways

1. Enterprise AI sales involve 6+ stakeholders with different concerns
2. The sales cycle is 12-24 weeks with distinct stages from prospecting to close
3. The business case is the most critical document — it must satisfy both technical and executive buyers
4. Proof of value is essential for AI sales — unlike traditional software, you often need to prove it works
5. Objection handling for AI requires addressing unique concerns about accuracy, explainability, and reliability
6. The handoff from sales to delivery is critical for AI projects — misalignment here causes failures
