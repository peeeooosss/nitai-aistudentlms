# Social Media Content Strategy

## Learning Objectives
- Design an AI-powered social media content strategy
- Create platform-specific content using AI tools
- Build content calendars and batching workflows
- Measure and optimize social media performance with AI

---

## Social Media Fundamentals in the AI Era

Social media has changed dramatically. The platforms reward consistency, authenticity, and value. AI helps you maintain all three without burning out.

### The Content Pillar Framework
Every social media strategy needs 3-5 content pillars — recurring themes that align with your brand and audience interests:

```
Example for a marketing consultant:
Pillar 1: Marketing tips and tactics (40%)
Pillar 2: Case studies and client wins (25%)
Pillar 3: Industry news and commentary (20%)
Pillar 4: Personal stories and behind-the-scenes (15%)
```

### Platform Differences
| Platform | Content Type | Best Length | Frequency | AI Use Case |
|----------|-------------|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| LinkedIn | Professional text + images | 150-300 words | 3-5x/week | Thought leadership drafts |
| Twitter/X | Short text + threads | 280 chars or 5-10 tweet threads | 1-3x/day | Thread generation, hooks |
| Instagram | Visual + captions | 150-300 word captions | 3-5x/week | Caption writing, hashtag research |
| TikTok | Short video | 15-60 seconds | 1-3x/day | Script writing, hooks |
| Facebook | Mixed media | 100-250 words | 1-2x/day | Group post writing |

## AI Content Batching System

The most efficient approach is to batch-create content: produce a week or month of content in one sitting.

### The Weekly Batch Process
```
Time: 2-3 hours per week
Output: 7-14 social media posts across platforms

Step 1: Choose this week's topics (10 min)
Step 2: AI-generate drafts for each platform (60 min)
Step 3: Human editing and personalization (45 min)
Step 4: Add visuals (AI-generated or curated) (30 min)
Step 5: Schedule using a tool (15 min)
```

### Platform-Specific Prompts

**LinkedIn Post:**
```
Write a LinkedIn post about [topic].

Structure:
- Hook (first line that stops the scroll)
- Story or observation (2-3 sentences)
- Key insight or lesson (the meat)
- Actionable takeaway (1-2 bullets)
- Engagement question (end with this)

Tone: Professional but conversational
Length: 150-200 words
Include: 3-5 relevant hashtags
```

**Twitter Thread:**
```
Turn this topic into a 7-tweet thread: [topic]

Tweet 1: Hook — surprising stat, bold claim, or provocative question
Tweets 2-6: Key points, one per tweet, with concrete examples
Tweet 7: Summary + call-to-action (follow, bookmark, share)

Rules:
- Each tweet stands alone (someone might only see one)
- No thread bombing — every tweet delivers value
- Use "1/" numbering format
```

**Instagram Caption:**
```
Write an Instagram caption for a post about [topic].

Structure:
- Hook (first line that shows "more")
- Value (2-3 paragraphs of useful content)
- CTA (save, share, comment, link in bio)

Length: 200-300 words
Tone: [casual/inspirational/educational]
Include: 20-30 relevant hashtags (mix of broad and niche)
```

## Content Repurposing with AI

The most efficient content strategy is creating once and distributing everywhere.

### The Repurposing Chain
```
Blog Post (1200 words)
  -> LinkedIn article (condensed 500-word version)
  -> 5-7 tweet thread (key points)
  -> Instagram carousel (visual summary)
  -> Newsletter section (curated highlight)
  -> Short video script (core concept in 60 seconds)
```

### AI Repurposing Prompt
```
I have this blog post: [paste post]

Repurpose it into:
1. A LinkedIn post (150 words)
2. A Twitter thread (7 tweets)
3. An Instagram caption (250 words with hashtags)
4. A newsletter blurb (100 words)
5. Three standalone quote graphics (short, impactful sentences)

Maintain the core message but adapt tone and format for each platform.
```

## Engagement and Community Building

### AI-Assisted Response Strategy
```
I received this comment on my post: "[paste comment]"

Write a response that:
- Acknowledges their specific point
- Adds value (not just "thanks!")
- Encourages further discussion
- Reflects my brand voice: [describe voice]
- Keeps it under 50 words
```

### Trending Topic Integration
```
This topic is trending on [platform]: [trending topic]

Write a post connecting it to my expertise in [your niche]:
- My take on the trend
- How it affects my audience
- What they should do about it
- A specific example or recommendation

Keep it timely and opinionated. 150-200 words.
```

## Measuring Success

### Key Metrics by Platform
- **LinkedIn:** Impressions, engagement rate, follower growth, profile views
- **Twitter:** Impressions, retweets, replies, link clicks
- **Instagram:** Reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks
- **All platforms:** Website traffic from social, leads generated, conversions

### AI Performance Analysis
```
Here are my social media metrics for the past month:

LinkedIn: [data]
Twitter: [data]
Instagram: [data]

Analyze:
1. Which content type performed best?
2. What posting times correlated with engagement?
3. Which topics drove the most interaction?
4. What should I do more of next month?
5. What should I stop doing?
```

## Key Takeaways

- Social media success requires consistency, value, and authenticity — AI helps with all three
- Batch content creation: produce a week of content in one focused session
- Each platform has different norms — use AI to adapt, not copy-paste across platforms
- Repurpose content: one idea becomes 5-10 pieces across platforms
- Measure what matters: engagement rate and leads, not vanity metrics
